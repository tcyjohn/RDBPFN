# Make mechanism survival the primary data-analysis target

The data analysis will primarily test whether R3's intended temporal, foreign-key, and signal-group mechanisms are realized in paired raw RDB instances and leave measurable signatures after DFS linearization. Benchmark alignment remains conditional supporting evidence. To preserve continuity with the original RDB-PFN analysis, the downstream signature will include its correlation-heatmap perspective, strengthened with aggregate cross-task statistics rather than representative visualization alone.
