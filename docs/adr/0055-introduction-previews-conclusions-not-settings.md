# Introduction previews conclusions rather than settings

The fifth Introduction paragraph summarizes the overall empirical conclusions
without presenting the experimental configuration. It states that the complete
prior improves compact-corpus prediction, narrows the observed gap to the
published relational checkpoint with approximately 99.9% fewer stored
relational tasks, and produces the intended mechanism signatures and positive
dimension-sensitivity evidence. Requested source databases, optimizer steps,
exact task counts, raw cells, AUROC values, intervals, and sensitivity-grid
numbers remain in Experimental Setup and Results.
