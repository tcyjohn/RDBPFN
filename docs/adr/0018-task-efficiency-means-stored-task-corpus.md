# Define efficiency in units of stored relational tasks

The paper title will use “Task-Efficient Relational In-Context Learning,” and the manuscript will define task efficiency as attaining a strong fixed-checkpoint result with a smaller corpus of stored relational tasks. Because SA-RDB-PFN increases raw rows in tables with schema out-degree at least one, the paper will not use the broader term “data-efficient” or imply reductions in generated rows, optimizer exposure, FLOPs, energy, or wall-clock time.
