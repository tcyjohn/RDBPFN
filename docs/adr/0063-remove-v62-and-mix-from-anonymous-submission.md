# Remove v6.2 and Mix from the anonymous submission

The anonymous submission will cover SA-RDB-PFN, RDB-PFN-Small, the four-point
cardinality/grouped-source sensitivity grid, and their unified evaluation and
analysis. We exclude v6.2 and both Mix variants from the paper supplements,
reproducibility package, manifests, and validation records so the submission
artifact remains focused on the main evidence chain and fits the venue's
50 MB code-and-data limit.
