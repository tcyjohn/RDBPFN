# Discussion leads with scale progress

Discussion and Limitations has three parts. It first presents the substantial
scale progress relative to the complete RDB-PFN schedule: approximately 99.9%
fewer stored relational tasks and 98.4% fewer raw cells while narrowing the
observed performance gap. It then interprets the DFS-facing prior design and
cross-stage evidence. Finally, it identifies the slightly lower mean AUROC than
the larger RDB-PFN checkpoint as the central limitation and connects that
remaining gap to future work on prior coverage and the relationship between
prior quality and corpus scale.

The compact-control raw-cell difference remains part of Results accounting but
does not organize the Discussion. Generic caveats about training seeds,
support-sampling intervals, matched quantities, and independently materialized
corpora are omitted in accordance with the PDF review.
