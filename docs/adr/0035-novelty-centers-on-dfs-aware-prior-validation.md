# Novelty centers on DFS-aware prior validation

The manuscript positions its novelty as relational-prior design and cross-stage validation organized around the DFS task interface: intended generator structure is connected to raw-database signatures, model-visible DFS representations where measured, and compact-corpus predictive evidence. Relational priors, HSBM relational generation, and synthetic-prior design are treated as established foundations; this positioning distinguishes the work from a broad first-prior claim and from a patch list relative to RDB-PFN.
