# ADR-0027: Separate raw mechanism evidence from post-DFS evidence in RQ3

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

RQ3 will be titled:

> What Structure Does the Prior Induce Before and After DFS?

The raw-database analysis will test whether all three intended mechanisms
produce their corresponding statistics:

- hierarchical foreign-key connectivity;
- calendar-aware temporal coverage and cross-table synchrony;
- grouped latent feature dependence.

Only grouped latent feature sources currently support the complete chain from
generator intervention, through a raw-database statistic and a post-DFS feature
statistic, to a focused predictive ablation. The manuscript will not imply that
the foreign-key and temporal mechanisms have each been independently traced
through DFS.

The cross-section summary claim will therefore be:

> The intended mechanisms are measurable in generated databases, while the
> grouped-feature dependence remains measurable after DFS.

This wording replaces broader claims that all induced structures or mechanisms
“survive DFS.”
