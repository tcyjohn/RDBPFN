# Limit the main data analysis to 1--1.5 pages

The main-paper data analysis will remain within 1--1.5 pages despite adding the entity-aware comparison. Roughly 0.8--1.0 pages are reserved for R2--R3 mechanism survival and DFS-correlation context, and 0.25--0.4 pages for v6.2 entity exposure. The paired-delta performance forest belongs in the ablation-results subsection and does not consume this budget. Mix receives only a brief composition/disclosure statement outside the data-analysis subsection; full diagnostics and sensitivity analyses move to the appendix.
