# Final polish follows four dependent passes

The manuscript-wide polish is performed only after the scientific structure is
stable, in four ordered passes:

1. paragraph purpose and internal logic, with each results paragraph following
   motivation or expectation, measurement or contrast, result, and scientific
   interpretation where applicable;
2. transitions and academic expression, integrating abrupt standalone or
   conversational sentences into their surrounding argument;
3. terminology and claim boundaries, consistently using `SA-RDB-PFN`, the
   four-dimensional relational prior, and source-row cardinality while removing
   obsolete R3, joint-removal, density-as-mechanism, inherited-pipeline patch
   language, and defensive negative declarations; and
4. grammar, notation, LaTeX, cross-references, citations, floats, and
   self-contained captions.

This order prevents sentence-level polishing from concealing missing paragraph
motivation or scientific interpretation, and prevents obsolete terminology from
surviving the structural rewrite.
