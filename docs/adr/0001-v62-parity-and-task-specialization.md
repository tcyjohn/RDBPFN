# Position v6.2 as aggregate parity with task specialization

The manuscript will not claim that the entity-aware v6.2 package outperforms data-prior-only R3: their mean AUROCs are 0.7072 and 0.7076, and the paired interval includes zero. Instead, v6.2 is positioned as preserving aggregate performance while producing a complementary task-wise profile; this matches the observed evidence and avoids turning a non-factorial comparison into a component-level superiority claim.
