# Disclose the source-aligned Mix while retaining the legacy checkpoint

The main aggregate table may retain the 0.7118 legacy-materialization Mix as a secondary highest-observed checkpoint, but a main-text footnote or limitation sentence will disclose that the source-aligned direct-R3 Mix reached 0.7079. Its full task-level and paired results move to the appendix, preventing selective reporting while keeping the legacy checkpoint from becoming the paper's focus.
