# Do not treat Mix as a third generator distribution

Mix is a fixed-weight training mixture of the v6.2 source and an earlier R3 materialization, not a separately defined relational generator. The data analysis will therefore characterize the two source distributions and their task-wise complementarity; Mix receives only a composition summary and its observed performance, avoiding redundant weighted-mixture statistics and unsupported causal claims about mixing.
