# Related Work follows four research trajectories

Related Work is expanded and reorganized into four trajectories:

1. relational predictive learning, from classical relational models and DFS to
   relational deep learning, benchmarks, and task-specific architectures;
2. relational foundation models, grouped by graph-centric, native multi-table,
   feature-materialization, and relational-ICL analysis paradigms;
3. relational database generation, distinguishing conditional synthesis from
   prior-based generation for foundation-model pre-training; and
4. PFNs and synthetic-prior design, including work on prior mixtures,
   generator coverage, and adaptive generator distributions.

The target reference list is roughly 35–40 functionally used sources. RT,
Relational Deep Learning, representative relational generators, Mitra, and
Robust Tabular Foundation Models are added. Foundation-model, HSBM, and SCM
foundations are cited in Introduction or Method rather than inserted into
Related Work solely to increase its length.
