# Abstract centers DFS-facing prior design

The Abstract first introduces relational database foundation models as
cross-schema, cross-task predictors facing scarce real pre-training databases.
Its central method insight is that a relational prior should align with the
information channels exposed through the DFS task interface: reachability,
availability, and content dependence, together with source-row cardinality.
This principle motivates the complete SA-RDB-PFN prior before the Abstract
summarizes cross-stage evidence and the approximately 99.9% stored-task
reduction. Experimental-setting details remain outside the Abstract.
